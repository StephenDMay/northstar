# Parked Ideas

Feature ideas that are good but not now. Added by `/ideas` when an idea is parked. When it's time, `/ideas` or a planning session moves one onto a roadmap and removes it from here.

Entry format:

```markdown
### <Idea title>
- **Parked:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **What:** one or two sentences
- **Why not now:** the reason from the discussion
- **Fits:** capability or milestone it would land in, if known
```
